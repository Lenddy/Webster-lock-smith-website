Right now you have a fixed border-radius: 2px 8px 8px 2px on both .page-front and .page-back. The goal is to make the inner edge (spine side) radius grow as pages stack up behind it, so it looks like the pages are actually physically stacking.
Before I write it, a couple of things to nail down:
The spine edge is the left side of the front face and the right side of the back face. As more pages turn (i.e. --c increases), the stack on the left grows — so the unturned pages' left radius should grow. Similarly the turned stack's right edge radius should grow.
The radius for each page should depend on how many pages are stacked on that side — which is either --i (pages turned before it) or N - --i (pages remaining after it).
One question before I write it — how pronounced do you want the effect? For example:

Subtle: max ~6px at the thickest point
Moderate: max ~12px
Exaggerated: max ~20px+ (very visible, almost rounded)
